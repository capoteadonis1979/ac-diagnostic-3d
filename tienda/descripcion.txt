AC Diagnostic 3D es un simulador de aire acondicionado para técnicos y estudiantes. Conecta el manómetro, arranca el equipo y mira cómo se comportan las presiones, las temperaturas y la corriente como en un equipo real.

• Tres equipos: aire de auto (R134a / R1234yf, válvula TXV o tubo orificio, compresor de embrague o variable), mini split (R410A, R32, R22, R290; on/off con capilar o inverter con EEV; 110 o 220 V) y aire de techo de camión 24 V.
• Manómetro analógico o digital, con sobrecalentamiento (SH) y subenfriamiento (SC) usando pinzas de temperatura.

• Herramientas de taller: bombona en báscula, bomba de vacío con vacuómetro en micrones y prueba de caída, inyector de aceite, recuperación y carga por peso.

Short title:
3D air-conditioning simulator: car, mini split and 24 V truck

Description:
AC Diagnostic 3D is an air-conditioning simulator for technicians and students. Hook up the gauges, start the unit and watch pressures, temperatures and current behave like on real equipment.

• Three units: car A/C (R134a / R1234yf, TXV or orifice tube, clutch or variable compressor), mini split (R410A, R32, R22, R290; fixed-speed with capillary or inverter with EEV; 110 or 220 V) and 24 V truck rooftop A/C.
• Analog or digital manifold, with superheat (SH) and subcooling (SC) using temperature clamps.
• Values checked against OEM service manuals and technical data: pressures follow outdoor temperature, humidity and altitude.
• Real faults: low or high charge, air in the system, clogged drier, stuck expansion valve, weak compressor, dirty condenser, dead fan, weak capacitor, open or shorted winding, overload (klixon), anti-freeze and more.
• Multimeter and clamp meter: volts, amps, winding ohms and capacitor µF.
• Shop tools: cylinder on a scale, vacuum pump with micron gauge and decay test, oil injector, recovery and charging by weight.
• 3D view and 2D view with the circuit diagram and large gauges.
• Works offline.
